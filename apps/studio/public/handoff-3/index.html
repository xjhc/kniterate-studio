2. Bind-off strip, Kniterate's settings

Strip 1 with three of Kniterate's settings, and nothing else changed:

  • The two circular rows after the zigzag (passes 109 and 110) at roller 200 instead of 400. Kniterate advises less roller pull when the first rows break; 200 is an experienced owner's value.
