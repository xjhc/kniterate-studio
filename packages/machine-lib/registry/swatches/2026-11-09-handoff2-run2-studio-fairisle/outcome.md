## What failed or changed

- **Cast-on corner:** on the first main-yarn row after the bottom waste, the
  yarn appeared cut or broken and frayed slightly at the bottom-left corner.
  The operator suspects too much pull; that is not confirmed. The cast-on
  passes (116 to 118) match Kniterate's own `fairisle.kc` (108 to 110) in speed,
  roller and racking.
- **Turning edges:** the edge where a carrier turns looks a little loose (the
  right-hand edge for a left-to-right pass). Studio's body matches Kniterate's
  file except six known passes where Kniterate's ground yarn also knits a
  needle the motif knits.
- **A file fact found afterwards:** the yarn catch makes a waste loop on
  rear-left needle 95 at pass 2 that the waste rows never knit again. It stays
  there until C2's catch knits it at pass 92, about 47 roller passes later, at
  the corner that broke. Kniterate's file and the first handoff have no such
  loop. It is a suspect, not a shown cause.
