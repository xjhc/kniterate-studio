# Cashmere A triangle · C3 decrease comparison

Use the 396-pass command.kc for this trial. The 432-pass control uses C4 on the front edge of each decrease. Both start from an empty bed with feeders at home on the left. Thread C1 with the usual draw thread, C3 with one end of Camel 3/28 cashmere, C4 with one end of Fig 3/28 cashmere, and C6 with the usual waste. Keep the same yarns and threading for both pieces.

The A is on the widening half. The piece starts on three columns, widens to 13, and returns to three. The body uses size 6, with the same ten C4 joined increases and ten inward decreases as the control. On the Fig face the outer stitch is Camel on each decrease and Fig on the following course. The color is reversed on the Camel face.

| Passes | What to watch |
|---|---|
| 1–168 | Eight-column setup, tapered starter, draw round and three-column cast-on |
| 169–301 | Three-column entry and all ten increases, identical to the control |
| 295–301 | Course 23 completes the final increase with Camel at the front edge |
| 302–309 | Course 24 makes the first decrease. The new edge is Camel again. Pass 308 is the first encoded pass that differs from the control |
| 310–367 | Subsequent edge colors alternate. The last decrease to three columns is passes 360–363 |
| 368–396 | Top waste, release, feeder exit |

Secure the six live cashmere loops before taking off the top waste. On both samples put the A on the same side, then compare the contrasting corner stitches on the two slopes. At the widest point, gently spread the two faces and check whether the edge stays joined and whether the two adjacent Camel stitches make a tidy turn or a loose loop. Inspect the reverse face too.

Software checks compared the actual compiled stitches on both beds. The 21 narrowing and finishing body courses change the outer column only. The two files share the first 307 encoded passes. The compiler schedules 18 fewer feeder moves and 18 fewer carriage-only moves. Both files contain 273 loop-forming passes and 40 transfer passes. The loop replay, both edge-join checks, the machine trace and the safety-envelope checks passed, with zero float-distance advisories. The hand and appearance of the cashmere edge require the physical knit.
